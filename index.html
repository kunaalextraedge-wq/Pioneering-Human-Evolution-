Kunaal Wachanekar

Human Performance & Transformation Coach

Kunaal Wachanekar is a human performance and transformation coach working at the intersection of physique development, strength and conditioning, nutrition, lifestyle optimization and behavioural change.

His approach goes beyond simply prescribing workouts or diets. Every individual is treated as a complete system — their goals, physiology, training history, lifestyle, nutrition, habits, mindset and environment.

The objective is simple: Create measurable physical transformation while developing the behaviours, discipline and mental framework required to sustain it.

From personal transformation and physique development to contest preparation, online coaching, nutrition consultation and one-on-one hypnotherapy, Kunaal works with individuals who want more than a temporary result.

His philosophy is built around understanding the individual first, then designing the system around them.
